## Key decisions made

- Keep only warrior, mage, and assassin as active archetypes while fleshing out equipment — the extra classes were there to explore balance, which is now better understood. Archived rather than deleted so they can be pulled back with a `git mv` out of `_archive/`. Reuses the existing `_archive/` convention from `assets/sprites/_archive/`.
- **Smith bonus is per-attack, folded into `power` (×coeff), not a new expression term.** Chosen over a separate additive `upgrade` term (which would have touched 13 shared damage expressions and needed a new StatExprEval var) — keeps THE LAW and every `.tres` untouched. Trade-off: the bonus is still ×coeff and per-hit, so `upgrade_scale` is a relative dial (final gain = `upgrade_scale × coeff × hits`); multi-hit attacks get a lower authored scale. `upgrade_scale` replaces the old global `POWER_PER_LEVEL` — one multiplier on `upgrade_level`, not two.
- **Bag stores instances, not bases.** Required to make smithing persist through unequip/re-equip. Kept the public bag surface base-facing so shop/rewards/display code didn't change — only inventory internals, two panel sites, and save/load.
