Parses + imports headless with no errors.
- **Fixed the "enemy alive at 0 HP" bug.** Not a stun interaction as guessed — it was **fractional HP with inconsistent rounding**. `health` is a `float`, and fractional damage sources (vulnerable/ward multiplier, `chain_multiplier` 0.5, `spirit * 0.5`-style expressions, armor overflow) left it on values like `0.4`. Three consumers then disagreed: the debug print (`%.0f`) rounded → log said `1`; `HealthBar`'s label (`int()`) truncated → screen said `0`; the bar fill showed the true sliver; and the `health <= 0.0` death check kept it alive forever. Adopted **integer HP everywhere** (rather than reconciling floats): round the applied delta at the `take_damage`/`heal` chokepoints and round `max_health` at every computation, so `health` is always integral and "shown 0 == dead" holds. Touched `scripts/enemy.gd`, `scripts/player.gd`, `scripts/health_bar.gd` (HP + armor labels now `roundi`). All three pass headless `--check-only`.

## Key decisions made

- **Lightning arcs to both flanks, not one.** The original design note said "one random adjacent enemy"; revised on authoring so a mid-line target sprays both neighbors (target `2` in `1 2 3` hits `1` and `3`). Kept the one-hop model — no further propagation/decay — and stayed within the existing single-target flow (the player still picks the primary; the effect reaches the roster via the scene tree).
- **Delivered as a spell on a mage weapon** rather than a melee attack or a proc, since mage weapons cast via `innate_spells` and `spirit`-scaled damage fits the caster identity.
- **HP is integral, rounded at application.** Chose to keep the `float` *type* (avoids churning declarations/save format) but round every delta and `max_health` so the *value* is always whole — over the alternative of keeping true floats and just making the label + death check agree. Cleaner combat readouts and a bulletproof "0 == dead" invariant, at the cost of a slight per-hit rounding shift in balance. Rounding lives at the `take_damage`/`heal` chokepoints so it covers attacks, chain arcs, and DoT ticks uniformly.
