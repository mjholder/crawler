## Key decisions made

- **Hybrid over bus-first.** The global bus fits reactive statuses but not these six — a bus-driven
  Fire tick desyncs enemy-borne burns (per-bearer ticking), and Bleed/Lightning/Brace are instant
  attack-effect logic with no signal to subscribe to. Built the subscription capability as
  foundation, used the fitting mechanism per verb. See [[design.md]] "Elemental & martial
  status-verb systems".
- **`apply_tick` hook over widening `Effect.apply`.** Gives a tick effect its `StatusInstance`
  (and thus `turns_remaining`) without churning every subclass/call site or changing the global
  `apply` contract; defaults to `apply`, so existing effects are untouched.
- **Pierce reduces only this hit's absorption**, leaving the rest of the buffer intact — pierce
  bypasses a hit, it doesn't drain armor.
