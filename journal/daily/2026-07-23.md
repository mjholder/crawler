## What I worked on

- **Pierce → percentage split.** Reworked pierce from a flat armor-ignore amount to a 0–1
  ratio split. `_apply_defense` on both `player.gd` and `enemy.gd` now does
  `to_hp = floor(amount * clamp(pierce, 0, 1))` straight to HP, remainder absorbed by armor
  normally (armor drains only by what it absorbs). Refreshed the `pierce_expression` doc
  comments on `damage_effect.gd`, `chain_damage_effect.gd`, `gated_bleed_effect.gd` (field
  kept as a String, reinterpreted as a ratio — no `schema.json` regen needed). The hit is
  rounded to a whole number *before* the split (in `take_damage`, after the incoming multiplier)
  so the shown damage and the HP/armor breakdown agree — a 14-damage Lunge at 50% now reads as
  7/7, not 6/8 off the fractional 13.8 that STR×0.6 produced.
- **Tooltip legibility.** `attack_preview.gd` now carries `pierce_ratio` on its `Line` and
  appends `· NN% armor pierce` to the damage line, flowing through `gui.gd`'s existing action
  tooltip. Feeds [[ideas/player-facing-legibility]].
- **Tooling.** Sidecar `linter.ts` warns when a numeric `pierce_expression` literal falls
  outside `[0, 1]` (migration safety net); content-editor `USER_GUIDE.md` documents the field.
- **Content.** `lunge.tres` migrated from `"strength * 0.3"` (flat) to `"0.5"` (ratio).
