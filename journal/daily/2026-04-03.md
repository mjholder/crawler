## Key decisions made

- `initialize()` is called before `add_child()` in `game.gd`, so CombatEvent stores raw data in `initialize()` and spawns enemies in `_on_setup()` (which runs after the node is in the tree). DialogueEvent and SkillCheckEvent do all their work in `initialize()` since they have no tree-dependent setup.
- Used inherited scenes for node subtypes: `world_map_node.tscn` is the base, `world_map_node_dungeon.tscn` inherits it and sets textures. Future SHOP/REST nodes follow the same pattern.
