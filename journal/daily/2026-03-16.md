## Key decisions made

- **Flat defense subtraction** — damage reduced by `max(amount - defense, 0)`. Simple and tunable; can swap to percentage-based later if needed.
- **Stats default to 50.0** — gives room to scale in both directions; placeholder until stat generation / leveling is built.
- **Damage formula: `base_damage + strength * 0.5`** — intentionally simple first pass. Equipment and other stats will feed in later.
