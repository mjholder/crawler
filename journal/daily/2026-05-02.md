## What I worked on

Designed Effect System v2: a comprehensive expansion of the existing `Effect` pipeline to cover three new dimensions — equipment passives (on-equip / on-hit / on-damaged / conditional), multi-turn statuses on both Player and Enemy (Poison, Bleed, Stun, Regen, generic stat buffs/debuffs), and run-long blessings owned by the Player. The load-bearing idea is a **lifecycle signal bus on `game.gd`** that publishes signals for every game-state and turn-state transition; statuses, blessings, and equipment procs all subscribe to whichever signals they need rather than each system owning its own tick loop.

No code changed this session. Decisions and rationale captured in [[design.md]].

## Key decisions made

See [[design.md]] — "Effect System v2: lifecycle signal bus, statuses, blessings, and equipment procs" (2026-05-02). Highlights:
- `game.gd` becomes a lifecycle signal publisher (turn start/end per combatant, event start/complete, attack hit, damage, death, consumable use, etc.). Subscribers connect and disconnect; `game.gd` does not consume its own bus.
- New `Combatant` base extracts buff/status state from Player so Enemy gains full parity. `BuffEffect` against an enemy stops being a no-op.
- `StatusData` resource carries `tag`, `duration`, optional `stat_modifiers`, `prevents_action`, and `on_apply` / `on_tick` / `on_expire` Effects. Stack policy is per-status (`REFRESH` / `STACK` / `MAX_DURATION`).
- `BlessingData` lives on Player (`_blessings: Array`). Carries optional `stat_modifiers` (summed into `get_effective_stat`) and a `subscriptions: Dictionary[StringName, Effect]` mapping bus signal → Effect. Acquired via `event.rewards.blessings` and `PlayerClassData.starting_blessings`.
- Equipment gains `on_equip_effects`, `on_unequip_effects`, `proc_effects` (`{trigger, chance_expression, effect}`), and `conditional_modifiers` (`{stat, amount_expression, guard_expression}`).

## Blockers / struggles

None this session. Risks to watch during implementation:
- Subscription leaks — every status/blessing/proc connection to `game.gd` must disconnect on removal/expiry/unequip. Phase 1 introduces a `Subscription` helper to make this mechanical.
- `apply_buff` does not call `_recalculate_max_health()` (`player.gd:399`) — CON buffs don't update max HP until the next equip change. Fix during the phase 3 migration.
- `buff_applied` / `buff_expired` are emitted but unwired in the HUD; replaced wholesale by `status_applied` / `status_expired` in phase 3.
