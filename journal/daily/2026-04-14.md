## What I worked on

- Tested level-up panel end-to-end and found two bugs
- Bug 1 (HP not refreshing post-level-up): `_recalculate_max_health()` in `player.gd` was silently changing `max_health`/`health` on level-up, stat-point spends, and equipment swaps without emitting any signal the HUD listens to. Fixed by emitting `stats_changed` at the end of `_apply_growth_rates()`, and updating `game.gd._on_player_stats_changed()` to also call `_gui.update_player_health()` (which also fixes the latent equipment-change case)
- Bug 2 (stat name labels blank until `+` clicked): `level_up_panel.gd` was using `find_child(..., owned=false)` in `_refresh()` to locate labels/buttons that had just been added in `_rebuild_stat_rows()` — the first lookup wasn't finding them. Replaced with direct references stored in `_stat_labels`, `_minus_buttons`, `_plus_buttons` dicts keyed by `Enums.Stat`, and seeded label text at row creation so there's no empty-text window
- Designed the Shop node type. Appended a `## ShopEvent` section to `journal/detailed/event-scene-design.md` covering node trees (event + panel), signal contract, phase flow, transaction routing, pricing, ShopData resource, WorldMapNode wiring, and signal-flow diagrams. Key design decision: all buy/sell validation and state mutation routes through `game.gd` — panel and event never hold player/inventory references. Logged in `design.md` as 2026-04-14 entry. ## Key decisions made

- Shop transactions route through `game.gd`, not the panel or event. `ShopPanel` holds no refs to `Player`/`Inventory`/`ShopEvent`. See `design.md` — 2026-04-14.
- `price` lives on `EquipmentData` as intrinsic base value; `ShopData` applies `buy_price_multiplier` / `sell_price_multiplier` per shop.
- Sell flow works on bag items only — equipped items must be unequipped first via existing InventoryPanel.
- Sold items re-enter shop stock (buy-back supported by default).
- Stock persistence across revisits is deferred; will live on `WorldMapNode` when needed.
