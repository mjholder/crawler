## What I worked on

Designed and began building the weapon-anatomy + item-instance + tag system from
[[ideas/weapon-anatomy-power-and-scaling]] and [[ideas/equipment-tags-and-riders]]. Plan staged
into three shippable slices. **Stage 1 landed and is verified.**

Stage 1 (two tunable damage axes, base data only):
- `WeaponData` gained `power`, `scaling`, and `scaling_stat` (one-stat-one-job).
- `StatExprEval` now exposes `power`/`scaling`/`scale` variables and takes an optional `context`
  dict; the three default to 0 so stat-only expressions and spells are unaffected.
- `Effect.apply()` and every override (`damage`, `gated_bleed`, `chain_damage`, `heal`, `buff`,
  `brace`, `status`) take a trailing optional `context` and forward it into `evaluate()`.
- `player.weapon_context_for(attack_data)` resolves which hand owns an attack → that weapon's
  `{power, scaling, scale, scale_stat}`. `game.gd._on_player_attack_hit` builds it once per hit
  and passes it into effect resolution.
- Shared/inline damage effects rewritten to ratio form `power * K + scale * scaling`
  (`damage_str_half`, `stab_damage`, `assassinate_damage`, `flail_damage`, plus inline effects in
  `lunge`, `palm_strike`, `flameburst`, `immolate`). Authored `power`/`scaling`/`scaling_stat` on
  the 7 damage weapons.
- `AttackPreview.compute` derives weapon context internally (no caller changes) so previewed
  damage matches the real hit; `humanize_expression` substitutes the vars so labels read
  `20 + STR × 0.5` instead of `power + scale × scaling`.
- Verified: headless import clean; a throwaway `StatExprEval`/`humanize` test passed all cases
  (composition, K coefficients, empty-context unarmed, labels).

## Key decisions made

- **THE LAW** (smithing→power, rarity→scaling) will be enforced in one place: the `ItemInstance`
  accessors (Stage 3). See [[ideas/weapon-anatomy-power-and-scaling]].
- **Neutral `scale` variable**: weapons declare `scaling_stat`; shared effects bind it, so one
  effect works on any weapon regardless of stat. Structural one-stat-one-job.
- **Stat-identity rebalances** (worth a designer look): rapier, spirit_fists, and vorpal_blade are
  now AGI-scaling; pyre_scepter is SPI-scaling. Vorpal mixes STR/AGI attacks but declares AGI, so
  its stab/assassinate rebound from STR to AGI. `unarmed_strike` stays pure `strength * 0.3` (it is
  an `AttackData`, not a weapon — no backing power/scaling).
- Riders, armor time-axis, mana-as-capacity, and LCK loot-roll odds stay **parked**.
