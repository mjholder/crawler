## Key decisions made

**Defense formula** — switched from `damage - DEF` to `damage * (1 - DEF / (DEF + 100))`. Diminishing-returns formula keeps DEF meaningful at all values, never reaches 100%, and never produces 0 damage on the player (floor of 1.0). Enemy floor stays at 0.0. Constant `DEFENSE_SCALING = 100.0` is tunable per-actor. Existing stat values (Warrior ~88 DEF, enemies ~12 DEF) produce 47% and 10% mitigation respectively without any .tres edits.

**Dodge** — separate pre-defense evasion roll driven by `Enums.Stat.AGILITY`. Formula `min(AGI * 0.003, 0.40)`. Assassin (AGI 65) gets ~19.5%; cap of 40% requires high AGI investment. Player-only for now. Emits a `dodged` signal; `game.gd` hooks it for a log message. Consistent with existing proc RNG (`randf()` in equipment.gd).

**Armor tiers** — no stat gating. Heavy/leather/mage identity comes from stat_modifier profiles and `spell_cost_multiplier`. Full mage kit stacks to ~0.65× spell cost. `spell_cost_multiplier` already existed on `EquipmentData` and was already applied in `compute_spell_cost()` — only needed new .tres values.
