## What I worked on

Reworked **burn** from a poison-clone into its own thing: a single-turn burst that discharges
its whole stack pool at the *start* of the bearer's turn, before it acts. 3 burn = 3 stacks →
on the enemy's turn start the health bar steps down 3, 2, 1 (total 6) with a short beat between
each, then burn is gone.

- `scripts/status_data.gd` — new `burst_on_turn_start` flag.
- `scripts/combatant.gd` — `BURST_STEP_DELAY` const + async `resolve_turn_start_bursts()`
  (walks `stacks` down, reusing `DamageEffect.apply_tick`'s `base × stacks`, emits
  `status_ticked` each step); `_tick_statuses()` now skips burst statuses. Added
  `_is_burst_bearer_defeated()` hook.
- `scripts/enemy.gd` — `take_turn()` `await`s the burst before `_perform_action()`; overrides
  the defeated hook via `is_dead`.
- `scripts/player.gd` — `begin_turn()` fires the burst too (enemy-inflicted burn on the player);
  overrides the defeated hook.
- `resources/effects/statuses/burn.tres` — `damage_expression` `"6"` → `"1"`, added
  `burst_on_turn_start = true`.

Then made the pyromancer actually stack burn: added multi-stack application so one hit can
grant N stacks (previously every application = 1 stack).

- `scripts/combatant.gd` — `apply_status(data, source, stacks := 1)` (STACK policy adds `stacks`;
  REFRESH/MAX_DURATION unchanged).
- `scripts/status_effect.gd` — new `@export var stacks: int = 1`, passed through to `apply_status`.
- `resources/attacks/immolate.tres` — burn `StatusEffect` now `stacks = 3` → a 3/2/1 = 6-damage
  burst on the enemy's next turn.
