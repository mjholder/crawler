## What I worked on

Added unique paper_doll layer sprites for the leather and mage armor sets. Each slot (helmet, chest, arms left/right, legs, boots) now has a class-specific texture rather than sharing placeholder art. Armor `.tres` resources for both sets updated to reference the new sprites. Plate armor files removed as part of the cleanup.

Replaced the defense and dodge formulas. Both `DEF` and `AGI` are now direct percentages — no scaling constants. `DEF 50` = 50% damage resistance, `AGI 15` = 15% dodge chance. The old diminishing-returns formula (`DEF / (DEF + 100)`) and `DODGE_AGI_FACTOR` constant are gone. See [[design.md]] for rationale.
