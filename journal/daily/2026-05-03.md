## What I worked on

Continued from [[daily/2026-05-02]] Phase 5 verification. After confirming the proc/signal fix, moved to Phase 6. Discovered that the battle axe's poison proc was occasionally landing on the player. Traced the call stack: `_confirm_targeting` builds `targets = [player]` for SELF-mode attacks (Brace), and `_on_player_attack_hit` was re-emitting the bus signal with that array unconditionally. The proc handler received the player as a target and applied poison.

Also fixed the warrior starting loadout — berserker ring was incorrectly placed in `starting_equipped` with key `6`; rings go in `starting_rings`.

## Key decisions made

`player_attack_hit` on the lifecycle signal bus now carries a "hostile hit" semantic — it does not fire for SELF-targeting attacks. This is enforced at the emission site (`game.gd`) rather than at each subscriber. If a future proc needs to react to self-buff actions, a separate signal (e.g. `player_self_action`) should be added rather than relaxing this constraint.

## Blockers / struggles

The bug was subtle because `_on_player_attack_hit` was doing two things: applying the attack's effects to targets (which legitimately includes `[player]` for Brace) *and* broadcasting to the reactive bus (which should only fire for hostile hits). The fix was to decouple the two concerns at the emit site.
