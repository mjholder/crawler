## What I worked on

Shipped both defensive reworks from the backlog, applied to **both** player and enemies,
with an armor HUD indicator for each.

- **Armor buffer (DEF).** `scripts/player.gd` + `scripts/enemy.gd`: added `armor`/`max_armor`,
  `refresh_armor()`, and rewrote `_apply_defense()` to absorb incoming damage from the buffer
  (overflow bleeds to HP; dropped the old `1 - DEF/100` percentage and the 1-damage floor).
  Player buffer refreshes in `begin_turn()`; enemy buffers refresh via a new
  `CombatEvent._on_round_started()` subscribed to `game.player_turn_started` (enemies take
  damage during the player's turn, so they need a round-start refresh from outside).
- **Dodge decay (AGI).** `Player._roll_dodge()` now halves dodge chance per successful dodge
  already made that round (`_dodge_streak`, reset in `begin_turn()`); only a *successful*
  dodge decays it.
- **HUD.** New signals `Player.armor_changed`/`armor_absorbed`, `Enemy.armor_changed`.
  Player gets a `PlayerArmorBar`/`PlayerArmorLabel` in `PlayerHUD` (`gui.update_player_armor`);
  enemies get an `Armor` overlay sprite on `HealthBar` (`set_armor` / `gui.update_enemy_armor`).
  A fully-absorbed hit deals 0, skips the hurt flash, and logs "Your armor absorbs N damage".
- **Docs.** New `design.md` decision + superseded the 2026-05-19 flat-percentage entry;
  marked both `ideas.md` entries shipped; updated `detailed/character.md` and
  `architecture.md` (Phase 11).

## Key decisions made

- **DEF is a refreshing per-round armor buffer; AGI dodge decays within a round** — shipped.
  Structural fix over tuning: armor absorbs up to DEF per round and overflow bleeds to HP, so
  immunity is impossible by construction; dodge halves per successful dodge to stop a high-AGI
  build no-selling a whole multi-attack round. Applied to enemies too for a symmetric, legible
  model. Named **armor** in code (the idea used "ward"). See [[design.md]] and [[ideas/def-refreshing-ward]]
  (2026-07-06 / 2026-07-05 entries).

## Blockers / struggles

- Enemies have no `begin_turn()` of their own, so their per-round refresh had to be driven
  externally — solved cleanly by subscribing `CombatEvent` to the `player_turn_started` bus
  signal, which fits the documented "game publishes, events subscribe" pattern.
